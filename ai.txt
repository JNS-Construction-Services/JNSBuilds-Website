EXTENDED SUMMARY
================================================================================

Homeowners hire JNS for work that needs a licensed certified residential contractor: kitchen remodels, bathroom gut-outs, room additions, framing, code corrections, water/storm-related repairs, and coordinated multi-trade renovations. Estimates start with a free site consultation and a written scope (inclusions, exclusions, and pricing) before demolition.

JNS is based in Palm Harbor and quotes licensed remodel work in Clearwater, Dunedin, Tarpon Springs, Safety Harbor, Oldsmar, and nearby Pinellas communities. New Port Richey and west Pasco are quoted when the job is licensed remodeling or construction, not a punch-list visit.
