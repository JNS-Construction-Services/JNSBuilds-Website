What we will not guess at on a Tarpon Springs house

Plaster over lath does not behave like drywall. A kitchen layout that looks cheap on a 1990s ranch can become a week of plaster patching here if we pretend otherwise. Out-of-square rooms mean cabinet scribes and filler that a production kitchen drawing will not show. We budget that when we see it, or we tell you the layout has to stay closer to the existing walls.

Sponge-dock tourism traffic on weekends is not a novelty; it is a scheduling constraint. We do not start a noisy demo window on a Saturday that the city or the neighbors will shut down. Anclote-adjacent and Spring Bayou lots can add flood-zone paperwork on an addition even when a kitchen stays inside the existing envelope. That paperwork is named in the proposal or we do not take the addition.
