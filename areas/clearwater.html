Countryside two-stories, Belcher ranches, Island Estates rules

Countryside and the two-story stock off McMullen Booth and Belcher are the most common JNS Clearwater calls: close the galley, open the island to the family room, replace a hall bath that has been leaking into the garage ceiling. Those houses were built as production plans. The work is layout, waterproofing, and finishes that can handle AC cycling and summer moisture.

Mainland ranches toward Coachman and downtown Clearwater often still have original baths and kitchens from the 1960s–1980s. Pan failures and undersized electrical are the rule, not the exception. We quote a full bath rebuild or a kitchen that includes the electrical correction instead of stacking a cheap cosmetic layer on a wet wall.

Island Estates and beach-side condos are a different product. Elevators, HOA quiet hours, dumpster bans, and parking for a crew can kill a job that looked simple in photos. We only price those after the rules are in writing. If the association will not allow the work, we walk away before a deposit. Baths, code corrections, and additions in Clearwater

Walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, and primary baths with a double vanity are licensed bath remodels.
