Main Street lots, Pinellas Trail, Skinner Boulevard

Downtown Dunedin lots are short on staging. A dumpster that would be invisible on an East Lake cul-de-sac can block a bike corridor or a festival weekend on Main Street. We walk the property, note where a truck can sit, and put that in the proposal. If the lot cannot take the container the scope needs, we say so before you pay a deposit.

Pinellas Trail corridor houses get the same honesty about parking and material drops. Marina / Skinner Boulevard properties add salt, wind-driven rain, and sometimes HOA or dock-adjacent rules that have nothing to do with the kitchen layout and everything to do with whether the job can run.

Dunedin kitchens and baths in small footprints

A Dunedin kitchen remodel is frequently a fight with inches: galley plans, a single window wall, and a desire for an island that the joists and the traffic path may not allow. We measure, we tell you if the island is a bad idea, and we design around what the house can carry. Cabinets, counters, lighting, and flooring still get specified for Pinellas humidity.

Baths in cottages are often a tub-to-shower conversion or a full pan rebuild in a space that barely fits a 30-inch door. Waterproofing and exhaust matter more than the tile Instagram photo. Details and photo proof live on renovations and upgrades and in the gallery.

If the Dunedin project is really an addition off the back — a primary suite, a larger kitchen bump-out — that is general construction, with setbacks checked on the visit.
