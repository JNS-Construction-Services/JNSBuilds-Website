Downtown Safety Harbor is not a Countryside kitchen clone

The spa district and Main Street commercial core sit against small residential lots. A kitchen remodel that needs a dumpster, a lumber drop, and a tile pallet has to share those streets with restaurant parking and weekend visitors. We will not copy a Countryside two-story price onto a downtown Safety Harbor cottage and hope the staging works. The visit decides whether the job can run in the window you want.

Bay-adjacent humidity is not a slogan. It shows up in bath exhaust, shower pans, and whether a closed-cell detail belongs behind a wet wall.
