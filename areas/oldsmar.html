Production homes, HOAs, and what Oldsmar kitchens actually need

Harbor Palms and similar 1980s–2000s subdivisions were sold with builder kitchens: a peninsula, a small island, or a galley that no longer matches how the family uses the lanai. Opening the wall is the request. Whether that wall is load-bearing is the fact. We check it on site. If it is, the scope includes the beam or the post, not a hope that drywall will hide the sag later.

School-run traffic on Tampa Road and Race Track Road changes when a dumpster can sit without becoming a neighborhood complaint. HOA architectural letters can delay an exterior opening by weeks even when the interior kitchen is ready. We put those gates in the schedule instead of pretending the subdivision has no rules.
